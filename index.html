    Measuring live

    1. Tap Start mic and allow microphone access.
    2. Hold the phone still, mic end (bottom edge) toward the source. Readings are a rolling 3 second average.
    3. Tap Stop. The capture stays loaded so you can drag on the time strip to pick a steady window.
    4. Calibrate once against a meter while live. The live offset is saved separately from the Voice Memos offset.

    From a Voice Memo

    1. In iOS Settings, open Apps › Voice Memos and set Audio Quality to Lossless.
    2. Record 15 to 30 seconds with the phone still, mic end toward the source. Don't touch it while recording.
    3. In Voice Memos, tap the recording, then ··· › Save to Files.
    4. Tap Load recording here and pick the file.
    5. For real dB, read a meter during the recording, enter its dBA and tap Calibrate.

    Phone mics roll off below about 100 Hz, so the 31.5 and 63 Hz bands read low. Leave Enhance Recording off. Without calibration all levels are relative (dBFS).
